How it works

  • Click a state to choose who visited, or edit an existing visit.
  • Switch to Cities to mark individual cities. A state with any marked city counts as visited.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Smaller cities appear as you zoom in.
  • Search opens visit details without changing your map view. Use a state name or abbreviation, or “city, state”.
  • Use ✎ beside a visited state or city to save when you first visited, a memory and a photo. Hover the state or city dot on the map to preview them.
  • Undo restores your last change. Save image exports your current map view as a PNG.
  • One shared map shows Alex in amber, Sam in violet and both in teal. Choose who visited when saving a place. This public edition saves visits only in this browser. It does not upload or share your travel data. Export a backup before switching devices or clearing browser data.

Year and traveler filters only change what you see. This map records first visits, not repeat trips.
